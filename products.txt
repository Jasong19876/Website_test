  HOW TO EDIT
  - Each product is one line. Columns are separated by  |
  - In stock: write  yes  or  no
  - To remove a color: delete its whole line.

    (see "PHOTOS" under each section).
  - The order of the lines is the order on the website.
  - Lines starting with  #  are notes and are ignored.
  - Changes show on the website 1-2 minutes after you save
    ("Commit changes" on GitHub).
